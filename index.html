What this means

Model: while serving, you keep serving on a win and switch to receiving in the same rotation on a loss. While receiving, a side-out win moves you into the next rotation to serve; a loss keeps you receiving in place. Each option is ranked by its exact win probability for a set to the target score (win by the margin above) — computed by working out every possible score sequence and its true probability, not by random trials — so it naturally accounts for the chain reaction into whichever rotation follows.
